How to Play

🎭 The setup

Everyone at the table is a clubmate — but a few are Fakers. Nobody knows who. One device gets passed around; your role stays secret.

✅ The honest side

On your turn, move around the clubhouse and finish club tasks. Fill the task bar to win — or figure out who the Fakers are and vote them out.

🕶️ The Fakers

Blend in. Pretend to do tasks. When you're alone with someone, tag them out — they become a Lounge ghost (ghosts can still finish tasks, so hurry). Trigger a crisis and run out the clock, or tag out enough clubmates that Fakers match the honest side — and the club is yours.

🚨 Meetings

Find a tagged-out clubmate and report it, or hit the emergency buzzer in the Lobby. Everyone reviews the case file — who was seen where, and when — then votes. Most votes is out. Choose carefully: vote out an honest clubmate and the Fakers are one step closer.

🏠 Between rounds

Earn XP and Club Coins, level up, dress your clubmate, decorate the clubhouse, and write your own scenarios in the Studio.
